# Ideas (not in scope — log here instead of building)

- Queued extractBid batch runs (a `job_kinds` row + worker handler) for whole-package or whole-job extraction with progress (SPEC §8.7 "long jobs", §11.4 sealed hold). Phase 1 runs extraction on demand from the leveling screen (`extract-bid`).
- **Opened items must say what they are (Janelle, 2026-09-27).** When an invoice, bill or any record pulled from another area opens in a box, it isn't always clear what was opened or where it came from. Every opened item needs a plain header: what it is (type + number), which job, and where it came from, before the content. Design pass before invoicing is built.
- **Calendar and the message board together (Jesse, 2026-09-27).** With the calendar added, the board could slide below the calendar in the main area. Jesse isn't sure that works yet; revisit in the Phase 2 calendar design.

- **Bids: make a report or package and send it out (Jesse, 2026-09-27).** The bids tool has no way to put a report or package together (e.g. a bid summary for the owner, or a package to a sub) and send it. No plan existed; talk with Jesse and Matt about what goes out, to whom, and in what form.
- **Folders that fit the person (Jesse, 2026-09-27).** A job's folders should lead with what that kind of user uses most. Inspector on a DSA job: Plans, Specs, DSA 103 (T&I), CCDs. On other jobs: the T&I section of the structural drawings is what the permit needs. Other trades later, with Jesse; people add their own folders and say whether search and the AI read them.
- **Anything with a due date rises to the top; recurring duties are automatic (Jesse, 2026-09-28).** The board orders by due date (RFIs late / not opened are on top now). Next: weekly and monthly duties as recurring tasks, e.g. the inspector's DSA semi-monthly report every two weeks, built as a synopsis of the daily reports ("create a 155 based on everything"). Needs a scheduler (no worker host yet).
- **Bid documents for bidders (Jesse, 2026-09-28).** The invite link opens our hosted bid set: plans, specs, pre-bid RFIs, the pre-bid meeting agenda. Per document, a "Downloads" checkbox (view only when off). After award, a sub with a contract gets full access. A real no-download view needs page images made on a server (the worker) or a PDF viewer library (pdf.js, a new dependency); the browser's own PDF viewer always offers a download. Wait for Matt's bidding review, then decide.
- **Pre-bid RFIs move into the RFI log (SPEC §11.5).** A bid question sent to the architect becomes an RFI in the RFI module (it's a bidding job, so turn the RFIs module on for it then).
- **Check-out while editing (Jesse, 2026-09-28).** Like the old hosted-document systems: a document someone has open for changes is locked to others until they're done. Part of the live markup / collaboration work.
- **The GC's own RFI form (Jesse, 2026-09-28; SPEC §8.3).** A GC uploads the RFI form they've used for years and we turn it into fields in our flow; until then everyone gets our format with their logo.
- **Moving My Daily Reports over (Jesse, 2026-09-28).** Build here what MDR doesn't have; when we reach something MDR already does, Jesse says so and we take it from MDR (rewritten to this repo's rules).
- **Impact claims should reach the owner rep (SPEC §7.4).** PM / PE are told now. Telling the owner rep needs a capability that picks that role (a matrix change, Jesse's call).
