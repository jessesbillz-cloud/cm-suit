SECTION 28 46 21.11 - ADDRESSABLE FIRE-ALARM SYSTEMS (SYNTHETIC FIXTURE)

PART 1 - GENERAL

1.6 CLOSEOUT SUBMITTALS
A. Operation and Maintenance Data: For the fire-alarm system, in bookmarked PDF.
B. Record of Completion: Signed by the installer.

1.7 QUALITY ASSURANCE
A. Field Quality Control: The manufacturer's authorized representative shall inspect the installation and certify it in writing before the acceptance test.

1.8 WARRANTY
A. Extended warranty may be offered at the Owner's option for an additional two years.

PART 3 - EXECUTION

3.4 FIELD QUALITY CONTROL
A. Acceptance Test: Test the complete system in the presence of the Inspector of Record and the Fire Marshal.
B. Give the Fire Marshal at least 10 working days' notice before the acceptance test.

3.6 DEMONSTRATION
A. Train the Owner's maintenance personnel for four hours. Schedule training with at least 14 days' notice.
B. Where a voice evacuation system is provided, include two additional hours of training.
